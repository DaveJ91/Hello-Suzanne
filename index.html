What was new

25Nov

The Finance Bill completed its Dáil stages

Finance (No. 2) Bill 2026 finished Report Stage and Final Stage in Dáil Éireann, carried 86 votes to 67. The Bill, first published on 19 October, contains the legislative foundation for structured domestic e-invoicing and real-time digital reporting. It now goes to the Seanad and has not yet been enacted.

Sources: Oireachtas Bill page, the vote, and the Official Report.

24Nov

Revenue held its Dublin tax practitioner session

Revenue ran the scheduled in-person session at An Lár, 14/15 Upper O'Connell Street, Dublin, at 11.00. It is part of the communication programme announced in October, not a new policy announcement. Two business webinars are listed for 1 December, and a Limerick practitioner session for 8 December.

Source: Revenue’s eInvoicing sessions page, published on 12 November.

23Nov

A conference recap, not a rule change

Chartered Accountants Ireland published a note on its Annual Tax Conference, held on Friday 20 November. The piece records remarks on preparing for e-invoicing from November 2028, and a closing session by Revenue Commissioner Ruth Kennedy on Revenue's move toward Tax Administration 3.0.

Source: Tax and Technology.

What did not change

No new Revenue eBrief, Tax and Duty Manual update, or press release on e-invoicing was issued between Monday 23 and Thursday 26 November.

The last official package remains the 21 October implementation paper and the 22 October press release. Peppol is still the default network.

No new Commission e-invoicing or ViDA legislative announcement dated this week was found. The revised VAT e-commerce notes are dated 24 July 2026, with an addendum on 6 October.
